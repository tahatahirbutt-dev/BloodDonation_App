Blood Donor ISB – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Blood Donor ISB ("the app") helps people in Islamabad and Rawalpindi find blood donors and lets donors respond to blood requests. This page explains what information the app handles and why. The app is operated by Taha Butt ("I", "me") as an individual developer.

What the app is, and is not

The app connects people. It is not a medical service, does not check anyone's health, and does not replace a blood bank or doctor. Whether a person can safely donate is decided by the blood bank. In an emergency, call Rescue 1122.

The app does not use your device's GPS or location permission, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your information for advertising.

Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics). Google processes data on my behalf under its own terms and privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Data is encrypted in transit.

How long data is kept

Your profile and account are kept until you ask for them to be deleted. Blood requests stop appearing to donors when they expire (the requester chooses urgency, which sets the lifetime) or when you close them.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn "Available to donate" or "Show me in donor search" off in the Profile tab at any time.
  • Close or cancel your own requests in the app.
  • To delete your account and the data listed above, email me at the contact address below from the email address you registered with. I will delete it within 30 days.

Who can use the app

The app is for people aged 18 and over, which is the minimum age to donate blood. It is not directed at children.

Sharing contact details

When you tap "I can help", you agree that the requester sees your name and phone number. They could contact you outside the app. Only respond to requests you are comfortable helping with, and be careful with any request that asks for money.
